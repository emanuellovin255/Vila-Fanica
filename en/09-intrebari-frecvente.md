# Frequently asked questions — English

<!-- Traducerea lui date/09-intrebari-frecvente.md. -->

## Where exactly are you?

At 7 Primăverii Street, in Băile Felix, Bihor county. The villa is across the street from the
Apollo thermal baths — you walk out of the gate, cross, and you are there.

Băile Felix belongs administratively to the commune of Sânmartin, if you need that for an
official form.

---

## How far are the thermal baths?

Across the street. Not "ten minutes away" and not "nearby" — the Apollo pools are on the other
side of the road. You walk there in your flip-flops.

You can see it in the photograph taken from the villa: our parking, the street, and the baths
beyond it, all in the same frame.

---

## Do you have parking?

Yes — in the yard, guarded, with thirty spaces. Not on the street and not in a public car park
two blocks away.

In summer, in Băile Felix, this is usually the thing that is missing. Here it is included.

---

## What is in the room?

Its own balcony with a table, a private bathroom with a shower, air conditioning, a television
with cable, a fridge and a hair dryer. The bed is a double, 180 by 200 centimetres. Wireless
internet reaches the whole house.

All rooms have the same fittings — there is no room "without a balcony" or "without air
conditioning".

---

## How much is a night?

It depends on the season and on how many nights you stay. For longer stays the price can be
discussed.

That is why there is no fixed rate written on the site: message us on WhatsApp with your dates
and we will reply with the exact price for those days. The "Check availability" button prepares
the message for you — all you have to do is press send.

---

## What is the fastest way to reach you?

On WhatsApp, at 0740 454 064. It is also the phone number, if you prefer to call.

Any "Check availability" button on the site opens a conversation with your dates, the room and
your name already written into the message.

---

## Can we cook or use a barbecue?

There is a gazebo with a barbecue in the yard, free for any guest to use, with no fee and no
booking.

Rooms have a fridge but no kitchen. A restaurant is three hundred metres away and a shop four
hundred.

---

## How far is Oradea?

Nine kilometres. Oradea airport is about fifteen, and the Borș border crossing twenty-five.

Băile Felix is connected to Oradea by public transport, so you can get here without a car.
