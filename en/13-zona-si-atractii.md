## The Apollo baths, across the street

Distanța: across the street
Poza: strandul-apollo-vazut-de-la-vila.webp
Prima pagină: da

This photograph was taken from the villa. In the same frame you can see our parking, the
street, and the Apollo pools beyond it. That is the whole distance.

What it means in practice: you walk to the baths in your flip-flops, without the car. You come
back at noon, into the shade, and go again in the afternoon. With children, the difference
between "across the street" and "ten minutes away" is the difference between an easy day and a
day spent carrying things.

---

## What is inside the Apollo baths

Distanța: across the street
Poza: strandul-apollo-bazinul-cu-fantana.webp
Prima pagină: da

Outdoor thermal pools of several depths, slides for children and for adults, lawns with
parasols, terraces.

The water comes from the resort's springs, between 20 and 49 degrees at source and rich in
mineral salts. That is why Băile Felix is not only a summer bathing spot: it is a treatment
resort, recommended for rheumatism, joint pain and recovery after injury.

---

## Băile Felix, the whole resort

Distanța: we are in it
Poza: strandul-apollo-vedere-de-sus.webp
Prima pagină: da

The largest year-round spa resort in Romania, established as a resort in 1885, with springs
known since the eighteenth century.

"Year-round" is the word that matters: several pools are indoors and work all year, so people
come here in November and in February too. In summer it is the open-air baths; in winter it is
warm water under a roof.

## The water slides

Distanța: across the street
Poza: strandul-apollo-toboganele.webp
Prima pagină: nu

The slide area of the Apollo baths, with several tracks and a landing pool.

This is the children's argument, and it usually decides where the family goes. Being able to
walk there in two minutes means you can go twice a day without anyone taking the car out of the
yard.

---

## Nymphaea Aquapark, Oradea

Distanța: about fifteen minutes by car
Poza:
Prima pagină: nu

The largest indoor water park in Romania: slides, a lazy river, a wave pool, a sauna area and
outdoor pools in summer.

It is the option for a rainy day or a winter day, when the baths across the street are not a
choice. You can do it there and back in an afternoon.

---

## Oradea

Distanța: 9 km
Poza:
Prima pagină: nu

The county seat of Bihor, nine kilometres away, and one of the finest Art Nouveau centres in
Central Europe: the Black Eagle Palace with its glass arcade, Piața Unirii, Oradea Fortress.

It deserves a whole afternoon. You can get there without a car — Băile Felix is connected to
the city by public transport.

---

## The lotus lake and the Pețea nature reserve

Distanța: in the resort
Poza:
Prima pagină: nu

The thermal lotus of Băile Felix has been protected by law since 1932, as part of the Pețea
Brook nature reserve. It flowers in summer, in water that never freezes.

It is a short walk, and it is the thing almost nobody knows about the resort, even though it is
right here.

---

## The Bears' Cave

Distanța: about 90 kilometres
Poza:
Prima pagină: nu

One of Romania's best known caves, with laid-out galleries and the cave bear skeletons it is
named after.

It takes a day, usually as an organised trip — you leave in the morning and come back in the
evening. It combines well with a stop in the Beiuș area.
