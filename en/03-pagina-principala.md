## Povestea noastră

Eticheta: Welcome
Titlu: Two steps from the water, with your car in the yard

The villa is at 7 Primăverii Street, facing the Apollo baths. Not "close to", not "a few
minutes away" — across the street. You walk out of the gate, cross, and you are there.

Every room has its own balcony and bathroom, air conditioning, cable TV, a fridge and a hair
dryer. The bed is a double, 180 by 200 centimetres. Wireless internet reaches the whole house.

In the yard there is a gazebo and a barbecue that any guest can use, and guarded parking with
thirty spaces — which in Băile Felix, in July, is worth more than it sounds. A restaurant is
three hundred metres away, a shop four hundred.

## Feature-uri alternante

### The baths are across the street, not "nearby"

Poza: strandul-apollo-vazut-de-la-vila.webp

This photograph was taken from the villa. You can see our parking, the street, and the Apollo
pools beyond it. That is the whole distance.

What it means in practice: you walk to the baths, in your flip-flops, without the car and
without carrying anything. You come back at noon, into the shade, and go again in the
afternoon. With children, you can go and come back as many times as you need.

### Guarded parking, in the yard

Poza: vila-fanica-fatada-de-sticla.webp

Thirty spaces, guarded, inside the villa's yard. Not on the street and not in a public car
park two blocks away.

In Băile Felix, in July and August, this is what everyone arriving by car is looking for and
almost nobody finds. You can see it in the photo from the baths, too: the cars are in the
yard, not on the road.

### A balcony in every room

Poza: balcoane-cu-muscate-vara.webp

Not in some rooms, not "on request" — in all of them. With a table and chairs, with geraniums
in the planters, on all three floors.

It is where you drink your coffee in the morning, before the pools open, and where you sit in
the evening once it has cooled down. On a summer holiday, your own balcony is worth more than
ten extra square metres inside.
